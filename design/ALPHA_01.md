# Alpha 0.1 — Vertical Slice

## Success criterion
Four players can pick up controllers, understand the game within a minute, create memorable chaotic moments, and immediately want a rematch.

## Playable roster
Four contrasting test archetypes:
1. all-rounder
2. speed/mobility
3. heavyweight/grappler
4. agile/technical

## Stage 01 — Runaway Train
One continuous fight:
1. Station departure
2. Countryside/canyon
3. Bridge crossing
4. Tunnel chaos
5. Damaged locomotive finale

Initial greybox uses three connected gameplay zones: Cargo, Passenger and Engine. Roofs are primary combat surfaces; selected interiors and connectors add elevation/choke points.

## Stage 02 — Sinking Pirate Ship
1. Full ship
2. Under attack
3. Structural damage
4. Flooding/listing
5. Wreckage finale

## Deferred
Online multiplayer, progression, campaign, cosmetics, final art, large roster and ranked systems are deliberately outside Alpha 0.1.
