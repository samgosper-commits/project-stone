# Alpha Playtest Protocol

## Question 1 — movement
Can a new player understand movement, jump and dodge in under 30 seconds without instruction?

## Question 2 — contextual intent
When Attack or Action chooses a target/object, does the result match what the player expected?

Log every "wrong target" moment. Targeting should feel invisible when correct.

## Question 3 — camera
With four players at maximum useful separation:
- can each player instantly locate themselves?
- can they see incoming hazards?
- does zoom ever make characters too small?
- does camera movement alter perceived stick direction?

## Question 4 — chaos quality
Separate good chaos (unexpected interactions and reversals) from bad chaos (loss of control/readability).

## Question 5 — stones
Does acquiring a stone change opponent behaviour? Does the third-stone threat create an obvious contest before transformation?

## Initial tuning variables
Movement: 8.5 m/s; acceleration 34; jump 9; gravity 24.
Dodge: 0.24 s at 13 m/s with temporary invulnerability.
Combo: 3 contextual strikes, 0.7 s reset.
Transformation: 12 s placeholder.
These are prototype values, not claims about original PS2 values.
