# Experimental Combat — Alpha

All mechanics here are removable. They must improve four-player decisions without increasing control burden.

## Perfect Dodge
Directional dodge remains the defensive baseline. During an early precision window, an incoming hit triggers Perfect Dodge.
Reward: brief counter-ready state, not an automatic attack. No conventional block.

## Contextual Counter
Attack during counter-ready performs a quick character/archetype-specific reversal. Action during the same window may favour reposition/grab. Alpha begins with a generic counter before roster-specific versions.

## Branching Combo
One Attack button. Repeated attacks form a basic 3-hit string. Movement state changes branches:
- neutral string
- moving attack
- aerial attack
- post-dodge attack
- counter attack
- contextual environmental follow-up

## Stone Resonance
Holding 1–2 stones provides a small archetype-flavoured benefit. Alpha placeholder: each stone modestly improves movement/attack responsiveness. Final effects must be character-specific and non-snowballing.

## Stone Burst
Power while untransformed and holding at least one stone sacrifices one stone for an emergency radial escape. This competes directly with saving three stones for transformation.

## Power Clash
Deferred until transformed combat is stable. Simultaneous major Power attacks may create a short timing/position clash while other players remain free to interfere.

## Rule
More decisions, not more buttons.
