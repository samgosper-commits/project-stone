# Falcon — concept direction 01

Status: proposed visual direction, not user-approved final design. Concept art is not a finished or rigged 3D asset. No game build changes in this design pass.

## Design intent

A recognisable modern Edward Falcon for Project Stone: an energetic pilot-adventurer and boxer, with the colourful chunky readability established in VISUAL_BIBLE.md. Keep his youthful expression, compact athletic proportions and confident stance. Modernise construction and rendering while preserving character identity.

## Identity anchors

Blond hair, pilot goggles, red flight jacket and trousers, white cravat, brown gloves and boots. Preserve recognisable costume colour blocking and broad hands/feet. Reference description: https://powerstone.fandom.com/wiki/Edward_Falcon . Powered-form artwork visually inspected at https://www.creativeuncut.com/gallery-20/ps-red-whirlwind.html : red rounded armour, prominent helmet fin, dark forehead panels, exposed metallic lower face, oversized silver knuckles and cuffs, rocket hardware.

## Proposed update

- **Face:** expressive eyes and brows, swept blond hair, self-assured smile. Keep facial planes stylised and readable.
- **Clothing:** rich red flight fabric with reinforced seam panels, cream collar lining, a compact white cravat and restrained brass fastenings. Give the jacket room for overhead crate lifts and a boxing guard.
- **Hands and feet:** substantial brown leather gloves and boots with clear finger articulation and broad soles. Small construction details should disappear cleanly when the camera pulls back.
- **Silhouette:** broad upper torso, defined waist, relaxed trousers and strong boots. Avoid long trailing cloth that hides hand/prop contact.
- **Materials:** distinct matte flight fabric, warmer leather, bright lens reflections and a few metal highlights. Use controlled wear on edges; preserve saturated colour blocks.
- **Powered form:** evolve Red Whirlwind's rounded red and silver shapes, helmet fin and huge gauntlets. Use a compact rocket pack, readable shoulder vents and bold joint breaks. Keep the shapes simple enough to read in a four-player battle.

## Gameplay connection

The manual identifies Falcon as a balanced fighter with a double jump; see GAMEPLAY_REFERENCE.md for the source. Proposed animation direction: springy boxing guard, decisive turns, clear jump tuck and a distinct second-jump silhouette. Prop lifting, targeted throwing, directional throwing and catching need readable hand contact. Powered actions should visually connect gauntlets, rocket hardware and directional force.

Avoid hiding weak mechanics behind long animations. Contact/release markers must match gameplay events, and locomotion must respond immediately. Timings remain provisional until real footage can be inspected.

## Concept sheet specification

One landscape presentation sheet: large normal-form three-quarter full body, smaller back view and expression detail, and a powered-form study. Warm off-white background, generous negative space, red/cream/brown/steel palette swatches. Polished stylised 3D game-character rendering with clean cel-like value grouping. Labels: FALCON, CONCEPT 01, NORMAL, POWER CHANGE. This is a visual exploration, not a dimensionally verified modelling turnaround.
