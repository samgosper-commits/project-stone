# Visual Bible — Alpha 0.1

## North Star
An HD 3D evolution of late-1990s/early-2000s arcade character art: colourful, chunky, expressive and immediately readable from an elevated isometric camera.

## Character rule: familiarity first
Legacy-inspired characters must preserve their recognisable:
- facial identity and expressions
- silhouette and body proportions
- signature hairstyle
- costume shapes and colour blocking
- personality and stance

Updates are evolutionary: richer materials, additional costume construction detail, improved facial animation, secondary cloth/hair motion and modern lighting. Do not redesign characters into generic contemporary anime archetypes.

## Rendering
- Stylised HD 3D; no pixelation and no photorealism
- Strong hand-authored/cel-shaded forms
- Saturated but controlled palette
- Bold silhouettes readable at gameplay distance
- Effects use graphic shapes before particles
- Materials remain simple enough that fighters dominate the arena

## Environment
Stages are colourful physical playgrounds rather than backgrounds. Props must communicate whether they are traversable, breakable, throwable or hazardous.

## Camera readability
Character value/colour contrast must survive zoom-out with four players and heavy environmental action.

## Alpha reference stages
### Runaway Train
Warm timber/painted metal carriages against bright expansive landscapes. Movement of the world sells speed while the playable surfaces remain readable.

### Sinking Pirate Ship
Warm timber and fire against cool ocean/storm values. Damage and flooding progressively alter navigation while maintaining player readability.
